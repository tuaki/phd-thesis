\chapwithtoc{Preface}

This thesis summarizes the results of the author's doctoral research in the field of multi-model data management. The research was conducted at Charles University in the Adaptive Data Management (ADaM) research group, particularly in collaboration with  doc. RNDr. Irena Holubová, Ph.D., and Ing. Pavel Koupil, Ph.D. Some of the results are the product of collaboration with members of the Scalable Database Systems research group, led by Prof. Dr. Stefanie Scherzinger at the University of Passau. The author also participated in supervising several related master's theses~\citep{srutkova-mastersthesis, strobl-mastersthesis, jezek-mastersthesis, mazourek-mastersthesis}.

The central claim of this thesis is that multi-model data management can support self-adaptation by combining a category-theoretic representation of schemas and mappings with evolution propagation, workload-aware configuration search, and performance estimation. This claim is supported through the results presented in a collection of ten papers published in peer-reviewed conferences and journals in the years 2022--2026. \Cref{tab:papers} provides an overview of these papers and their roles in the thesis. A full \hyperref[chap:publications]{list of the author's publications} is included at the end of the thesis.

The thesis builds on previous work by the research group. Specifically, this theoretical foundation includes a unified categorical representation, transformations, schema inference, and querying. The original contribution then lies in evolution management, workload-aware mapping selection, supporting benchmarking infrastructure, and schema-free query unification. For each of these topics, we identify research challenges, propose solutions, and discuss the contributions of the individual papers.

This research was supported by the Czech Science Foundation (GAČR) grants no.~20-22276S and~23-07781S, the Charles University Grant Agency (GAUK) grant no.~292323, and the Bayerisch-Tschechische Hochschulagentur Akademische Projekte grant no.~BTHA-AP-2023-21.
